# Code of Conduct

Flow follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the people using Flow. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report a problem, use GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/thenetaji/flow-music/security/advisories/new) form or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately, and maintainers may remove content or block people who break these rules.
